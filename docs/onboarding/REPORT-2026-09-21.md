| when | what a fresh account saw |
|---|---|
| 0–2 s | Daily Calendar auto-opened as a blurred modal; 0.5 s later the tutorial card glided in **under** it. Goals bar led with "Catch an Uncommon+ brainrot 0/2" — impossible on Pebble (25 studs; Uncommon starts at 100). |
| intro | "WELCOME TO SKIP A BRAINROT" + two paragraphs + LET'S GO (20 s AFK auto-advance). |
| step 1 | CLAIM YOUR BASE — walk ~105 studs to a plot. |
| step 2–3 | SKIP A ROCK / CATCH A BRAINROT at the dock (~100 more studs). Every throw catches; 100 % Common under 100 studs. Distance never printed. |
| step 4–5 | PUT IT ON YOUR BASE ("open your Bag and tap") / COLLECT at the campfire — walk back. |
| step 6 | SELL one brainrot at Marco (≈ $1,150). |

Roughly 3–5 minutes of walking/reading for 1–2 throws, plus the 90 s stall.

## 3. Exact tutorial changes (`TutorialClient`, rewritten)

- Intro paragraph + LET'S GO removed; the first card is the first action.
- Ladder: SKIP YOUR FIRST ROCK → IT'S IN YOUR BAG (catch 5) → GOAL COMPLETE (claim own5) →
  OPEN YOUR BAG → PUT THEM ON YOUR BASE → COLLECT YOUR CASH → BUY A BETTER ROCK → YOU'RE SET.
  CLAIM A BASE exists only as a fallback when the server could not auto-claim (full server).
- Removed from the mandatory ladder: SELL, GRAB UPGRADES (Enzo), TALK TO ELDER TUNG.
- **"Skip This Step" removed** (no step can dead-end); Skip Tutorial kept on every card.
- Every step is gated on a real event or the profile snapshot (`Brainrots` count, `PadIndex`,
  `OwnedRocks`, `TotalEarned`, the goal's `Claimed` flag, `HudOpenPanel == "bag"`), never a
  timer; the ladder fast-forwards past already-satisfied steps on join (rejoin resume).
- The card never changes while a catch is being revealed: it waits for the reveal to end
  (`ThrowCamBusy` / `RevealPopActive` attributes from the reward-pacing lane when present,
  else a 1.2 s grace covering the Common pop).
- New: gold pulsing ring on the Bag / Goals rail tiles for UI-targeted steps; the cue line
  shows each skip's distance → tier; copy reads the goal reward, the rock name/price/reach
  multiple and the pad count from config, not literals.
- New: `OnboardingActive` player attribute (true while running, false on finish/skip) —
  the calendar handshake.
- New: one-shot post-tutorial "spares sell at Marco's" toast when the base is full.
- Phone: compact 300×120 card top-left under the currency cluster (see the test log for
  the simulator result).

## 4. Exact UI sequencing changes

- `GameHUDClient` join sequence: the calendar's auto-open is **held** while the join
  snapshot says `TutorialDone == false`, released when `OnboardingActive` drops (finish or
  skip) or by a 10-minute ceiling; the held `DailyState` payload replays unchanged.
  Returning players: byte-for-byte the same behaviour as before (opens at join).
- `Main`: a first-join profile (`TutorialDone == false`, no valley camp) gets the free valley
  plot nearest the spawn claimed for it on join — no teleport, no toast
  (`ClaimPlotFor(player, plot, quiet)` grew an optional flag; every other caller unchanged).
- `QuestHandler`: `own5` authored first; tier goals report `Cur = 0` until met (a Common is
  not "1 of 2" toward an Uncommon).
- `QuestBarClient` (live round-3 file, now mirrored): a board with nothing claimed follows
  the authored order (a met goal still wins); tapping the bar while READY! opens Goals.
- `GoalsPanel`: opens on the GOALS tab whenever a one-time goal is claimable (also on the
  first QuestState push after a lazy first open); a tab the player picked is never overridden.
- `MerchantClient`: rock cards say what they reach — second info line
  "Catches <TIER> • <Next> from <N>m" from the world's rarity ladder; two-line info at 14 px;
  `INFO_H` 34 → 50.

## 5. Economy values changed, and why

| value | before | after | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `RockConfig` Rivertumble (`SmoothStone`) price | 12,000 | **2,000** | The only economy number touched. The header's own intent is "first purchase quickly"; 12,000 was ~10× a fresh profile's cash at that step. 2,000 is the own5 goal reward (2,400) minus a little, so five catches + one claim buy it at ~4 min without depending on the daily or on selling. Still 100 % Common (70 < 100 studs) — it sells 3× reach, 4 skips and the tracking camera; Sevenskip (23,900) stays the first tier unlock and is the named next target. |

Unchanged on purpose: `TUTORIAL_REWARD` 3,000, every goal reward, sell multiplier, daily
rewards, upgrade prices, rarity bands, every other rock.

## 8. Still needs a larger progression decision

1. **The bottom of the rock ladder buys no rarity.** Pebble (25) and Rivertumble (70) are
   100 % Common; the first Uncommon chance is 4–7 % on Sevenskip ($23,900), 16–20 % on
   Boulderfang ($287,000). "Why better rocks matter" is now *stated* on the cards and in the
   finish copy, but the first $36K of purchases still changes nothing a player can feel
   except reach. Options: give Rivertumble/Sevenskip a small Uncommon reach (e.g. Sevenskip
   MaxDistance 110 → 130) or reprice Sevenskip; both are ladder decisions.
2. **Elder Tung is not a first quest.** His hand-over (5 Epic + 5 Legendary + 2 Mythic) is
   hours away; he is discoverable in the world now, not tutorialised. A genuine early NPC
   quest (e.g. "bring Tung 3 Commons") would need quest design.
3. **`rocks5` / `rocks10` goals mislabel their target**: desc/target say 5 and 10 different
   rocks, `met` requires 7 and 14 (`QuestHandler`). The bar can read 5/5 without READY!. Not
   touched (payout timing is an economy call).
4. **Goals board vs starter progress**: "Own 5 different rocks" counts the free Pebble (and
   each world's free starter). Now hidden behind the fresh-board rule until the first claim,
   but the fraction rule returns afterwards.
5. **Distance is not shown in the default reveal** (only the tutorial cue and the legacy
   ResultFrame print it). A small "Nm" on the skip counter's last tick would make "farther =
   rarer" visible for the whole game; it is HUD work outside this round.
6. **Selling as the early income** (a Common sells for ~15 min of its own income) is taught
   by one toast and the finish copy only. If the owner wants it in the ladder, a SELL A SPARE
   step after the base is full is the natural place.
7. **A first-minute Chim** (1-in-250 AnyTier roll, Mythic, ~31 K/s placed) happened in one
   of four runs and trivialises the first hour. Working as designed; worth a look at the
   AnyTier floor for profiles under a few minutes old.
8. **Shared-Studio hygiene**: `ProfileKeySuffix` must be nil in the place file; one lane's
   test helper fired into another lane's Play session this round — tagging sessions with a
   workspace attribute and announcing START/STOP is now the agreed protocol.
